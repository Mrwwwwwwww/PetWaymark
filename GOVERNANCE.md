# Governance / 治理草稿

The project owner appoints maintainers before public launch. No regional reviewer has been recruited or assigned by this scaffold. Maintainer roles, release permissions and affiliations must be documented when real people accept them.

The lead maintainer manages scope and releases. Qualified regional reviewers verify original evidence. Contributors may propose corrections without being transport providers. Rules are never approved by AI alone. Conflicts remain visible and affected conclusions stay blocked until resolved; keep a decision and version trail.

Initial proposed cadence: weekly issue triage, monthly coverage and maintenance reports; review carrier/facility records every 30 days and stable regulatory records every 90 days, sooner on announced changes. These are internal goals, not freshness guarantees or staffed service commitments.

Apply the same evidence and ranking standards to all providers, including Petzod / 宠云际. Disclose affiliations and recuse conflicted reviewers. Ordinary corrections can use future issue templates; security and personal data follow SECURITY.md.
