# Governance / 治理草稿

The project owner appoints maintainers before public launch. No regional reviewer has been recruited or assigned by this scaffold. Maintainer roles, release permissions and affiliations must be documented when real people accept them.

The lead maintainer manages scope and releases. Qualified regional reviewers verify original evidence. Contributors may propose corrections without being transport providers. Rules are never approved by AI alone. Conflicts remain visible and affected conclusions stay blocked until resolved; keep a decision and version trail.

Initial proposed cadence: weekly issue triage, monthly coverage and maintenance reports; review carrier/facility records every 30 days and stable regulatory records every 90 days, sooner on announced changes. These are internal goals, not freshness guarantees or staffed service commitments.

PetWaymark is a purely public-benefit open-source project that helps pet owners, free and open and unaffiliated with any commercial brand. Any compliant service provider may connect on equal terms through open interfaces. The project does not operate transport or other commercial services, accept orders or favor any provider. Service connections link or redirect users to providers' own channels.

Apply the same inclusion, evidence, ranking and interface access standards to all providers, with no preferential placement or exclusive access. Disclose affiliations and recuse conflicted reviewers. Ordinary corrections can use future issue templates; security and personal data follow SECURITY.md.

项目以帮助宠物主人为目的，纯公益、免费开放，与任何商业品牌无关。任何合规服务商都可平等通过开放接口接入；项目不经营、不收单、不偏向任何商家，服务对接／跳转到服务商自有渠道。统一收录、证据与排序标准，继续执行利益披露、回避、合规和安全要求。

首发前由项目所有者明确实际维护者与私密报告渠道；当前不虚构人员、联系方式、复核签名或服务时限承诺。
