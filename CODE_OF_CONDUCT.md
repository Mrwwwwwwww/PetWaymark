# Code of conduct / 行为准则

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Welcome contributors across languages and experience levels. Critique claims and changes, not identities. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deceptive endorsements and unsolicited promotion are unacceptable.

Do not publish personal travel documents or customer data. Ask before quoting interview participants. State conflicts of interest and avoid unsubstantiated accusations against providers.

Maintainers may request edits, remove harmful material, restrict participation or reverse an action after review. Explain decisions where privacy permits. The owner must designate a private conduct-report and appeal channel before public launch; none exists in this local scaffold. Do not use public issues to share sensitive evidence.

尊重不同语言与经验的贡献者，以证据讨论问题。禁止骚扰、歧视、泄露隐私、冒充背书与营销刷屏。上线前配置真实的私密举报与申诉渠道。
