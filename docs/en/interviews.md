Keep unfilled directions visible. Multiple journeys by one person do not count as multiple participants. Include dogs and cats, aim for at least four small-town endpoints, two separate-date/authorized-companion experiences and one cancellation/refusal/abandoned trip. Quotas overlap and are not statistically representative.

Use several channels; avoid sampling only one provider's customers or relying solely on provider referrals. ## 35–40 minute guide

Opening (3 min): Explain that this is research for a planning tool, not order handling. Participation is voluntary; participants may skip questions or stop. Use anonymous notes and no recording by default. Obtain separate consent for recording, quotations or follow-up. Do not request passports, microchip IDs, addresses, booking references or information about other people.

1. **An actual event (5 min):** Tell me about your most recent pet journey, including one you prepared for but could not complete. City-level endpoints are enough. Dog or cat? Who traveled with the pet? What was the approximate timeline? Let them narrate first.
2. **Steps and difficulties (6 min):** What did you do first, and how did you choose the next step? Which step took most effort or required rework? Describe a specific recent incident and its consequence. Approximate ranges are sufficient; do not request documents.
3. **Information and trust (4 min):** Where did you look for answers? Did sources disagree? How did you decide whom to trust or ask? What remains unresolved?
4. **Door-to-door transfers (4 min):** How did you reach the departure hub and final destination? Who handed over the pet, and where did waiting occur? Did you compare another airport, rail or driving over several days? Why reject an alternative?
5. **Timing and cost (4 min):** Which documents or reservations depended on earlier steps? What charges appeared later? If you could solve one thing earlier, what would it be? Do not show the feature list yet.
6. **Comprehension task (6 min):** Show the synthetic text card below. Ask them to explain the status, next action and remaining confirmations in their own words. What is sufficient, and what might mislead them?
7. **Tradeoffs and close (3–5 min):** Rank the top three among document timeline, transfer comparison, cost breakdown, handover checklist and sourced explanations. “None” is valid. Why? What works better in the current alternative? Finally ask whether they would provide necessary dates/dimensions without uploading original documents. Avoid leading questions about liking the tool.

For professionals, adapt questions 1–5 to an anonymous rework case, commonly misread inputs, situations requiring human judgment, evidence needed for review, responsibility at handover gaps and time spent updating rules. Distinguish firsthand experience from hearsay. Do not request customer files. Keep questions 6–7 consistent.

## Synthetic comprehension card — not travel advice

“Huzhou → a departure airport → an overseas destination. Status: `unsupported`; destination-state rules have not been covered. Carrier acceptance is unknown. Total cost is unknown. You can organize pet and owner dates first; the system cannot currently determine feasibility.”

Ask: Does this mean you can buy a ticket? What is missing? What would you do next? Then compare text cards stating “reviewed conditions met, carrier confirmation still needed” and “dates missing, further input needed.” Record whether they mistake a status for a booking. Do not supply the answer until after their first response, then explain the intended statuses.

## Priority hypotheses and counterevidence

| Initial order | Hypothesis | Signal | Evidence that would lower its priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Document order and date windows cause major rework | Unprompted incident involving delay or repeated paperwork | Carrier acceptance dominates; trusted agents already solve documents |
| 2 | Small-town transfers and handovers matter more than the nearest airport | A farther hub reduced uncertainty or handovers | Participants need only one leg; ground transfers are already solved |
| 3 | Sourced explanations of blockers reduce repeated consultation | Participant can identify the gap and next step even when travel is unresolved | Only full-service help is useful; unsupported results offer no actionable value |
| 4 | Cost breakdowns are more useful than unsupported totals | Overlooked costs and specific comparison needs | Budget is not decisive or an inclusive fixed quote suffices |
| 5 | Users will provide minimum structured input without originals | They understand and accept necessary dates and dimensions | Effort or privacy concern exceeds perceived benefit |

Code spontaneous narratives before prompted rankings and keep them separate. Record denominators, severity (blocked/rework/inconvenience), alternatives and counterexamples; repeated mentions by one person count once. At least three independent concrete incidents can justify raising a priority; one serious risk can trigger separate review. This is an internal heuristic, not statistical significance.

## Blank record and Week 2 handoff

Fields to copy, without inventing responses: anonymous ID; consent scope; role/language/channel/affiliation; direction and city-level endpoints; species/accompaniment; completed or planned; key incident; problem tags; impact; workaround; spontaneous or prompted; first card interpretation; feature ranking; counterevidence; quotation permission; separate follow-up consent.

Keep raw notes outside the repository in restricted storage; publish only consented anonymized synthesis. Separate contact details from notes. If recording is later agreed, define purpose and deletion date first. Suggested default: delete raw notes and separate contact records 30 days after analysis unless an explicit alternative is documented.

Week 2 synthesis should state actual sample size and unfilled directions, the top three problems with counterevidence, input burden, misunderstood statuses and proposed scope changes. Without interviews, retain “unvalidated.” Recruitment and interviewing still require separate explicit authorization.
