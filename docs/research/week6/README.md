## 2026 documents / 文件版本

2026/705 applies from 22 April 2026. Annex I is the member-issued passport;
Annex III is the third-country dog/cat health certificate. Article 6(1) recognises
577/2013 passports issued before 1 January 2028; this is an issuance cutoff, not
a blanket expiry on that date. Article 6(3) recognises the old health certificate
issued before 1 October 2026 during a transition until 31 March 2027. Recognition
never extends ordinary document validity. Annex III notes use a 10-day issue-to-entry
check window (sea extension separately); onward movement is at most six months or
rabies expiry. Its titre note uses ≥90 days sample-to-issue, not sample-to-entry;
primary sample is ≥30 days after vaccination, titre ≥0.5 IU/ml, designated lab and
attached certified report. Full validity, boosters and endorsement are uncompiled.
The small helper checks model/date consistency only; it cannot validate a certificate.

## Exception and scope queue / 例外与范围

Readable tattoo must predate 3 July 2011; identification/vaccination order still
needs checking. Existing chips and young-animal permissions have distinct exceptions.
Listed-origin exemption, documented predeparture return, secured transit declaration
and uninterrupted booster continuity are separate titre branches. Country-list access,
2019/2035 identification/passport provisions, 2020/688 Annex VII and 2020/692 Annex XXI
are pending. No “three months = 90 days” substitution for historical instruments.
