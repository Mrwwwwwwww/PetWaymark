# 0006 — EU evidence scope before route construction

2026-10-08. Week 6 adds a shared offline evidence assessment, not an unverified EU
transport graph. The existing engine handles three cross-member draft constraints;
a small EU wrapper separates wholly domestic travel, classified cross-member movement,
model/date diagnostics and the 27-member evidence inventory. No facilities, schedules,
road distances or provider capability have been verified, so no operational route is
constructed. The CLI and loopback web invoke the same wrapper.

Do not apply cross-member law to entirely domestic travel. Owner-not-moving boarding
must stop before ordinary documents/routes even without an ownership transfer.
Neither cargo mode nor a consistent certificate model settles legal classification.
Only trusted repository inventory supplies transition dates and evidence; anonymous
input cannot approve rules, custody or bookings. Three historical third-country drafts
retain their IDs and scope, advance to revision 2 and point to the new research queue.
Three separate intra-EU drafts have their own IDs. This is a backwards-compatible 0.1.0
extension: new movement category, inventory schema and actual de/fr source languages.

Source text unavailability and lack of two qualified human reviewers prevent complete
framework/member acceptance. The local and future cross-border scope remain unsupported.
Article/annex differences, document validity and local duties must be reviewed before
any enforcement; previews and synthetic tests cannot substitute for that work.
No boarding workflow, transaction, contact, hosted service or paid runtime is added.
