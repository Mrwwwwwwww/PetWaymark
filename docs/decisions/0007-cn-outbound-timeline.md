# 0007 — CN outbound research timeline / 中国出境研究时间轴

Status: accepted design; no legal approval. Date: 2026-10-08.

Use independent dog/cat branches for CN→US and CN→EU. Keep the public package
unsupported until current origin/destination procedures and two actual qualified
human reviews exist. No forward-rule reversal and no synthetic reviewers.

The shared module produces a dependency timeline, draft diagnostics, entry-point
filters and original-document responsibility tasks, not an operational route graph.
CLI and local bilingual web use the same result; no paid runtime or remote services.

Dates have explicit semantic anchors. EU sample-to-issue is not sample-to-entry;
Article 19 official issue versus authorised issue/authority endorsement is not a
single anonymous “certificate date”. US foreign-vaccine form validity is measured
from veterinarian signature, not reset by government endorsement. Origin export
certificate is a distinct document. Proposed final appointments cannot replace actual
issue dates or silently bypass sample waits. Day bounds are research estimates;
UTC hour checks require offset plus IANA zone and a distinct onward-zone arrival.

ACF listing/entry designation, actual product acceptance, facility capacity/hours,
release and onwards transport are separate. SEA cargo-only cannot become a cabin
option. EU traveller points do not prove commercial inspection-centre capability.
Unknown first-entry points and local overlay gaps remain visible.

Only dates, conditions and anonymous role categories enter exported results. Caller
private identifiers and claims of review, booking or completed custody cannot
upgrade the package. Reported carrying/delivery roles remain pending actual arrangement.
No certificate upload, contact store, provider ranking, order, dispatch, payment,
quotation sending or receipt-signing flow is implemented.
